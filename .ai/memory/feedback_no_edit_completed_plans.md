---
name: No edits to completed plans
description: Never modify or reference-update completed/archived plans — they are immutable historical records
type: feedback
---

Do not edit completed plans to update stale references when new work changes what they describe. Completed plans are historical records — their Context describes state at planning time, not current state. New plans should not include Decisions entries about stale references in sibling completed plans either.

**Why:** The user considers completed plans immutable history. Adding cross-references or notes about future changes is unnecessary noise.

**How to apply:** When a plan-reviewer flags stale references in completed plans, dismiss the finding — do not add update sub-tasks or Decisions entries about it.
